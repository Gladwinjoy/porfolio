M2

The new BMW M2 (G87 generation) is a high-performance M model featuring a 3.0L twin-turbocharged inline-6 engine producing around 480 hp and 550 Nm of torque, paired with a 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic transmission

X7

Integral Active Steering in a BMW X7 is an advanced system that enhances vehicle maneuverability and driving stability by integrating both front and rear wheel steering

Z4

The BMW Z4 is a two-seat roadster known for its rear-wheel drive and powerful engines, available in configurations like the M40i with a 3.0L TwinPower Turbo engine delivering up to 335 bhp

i7

The BMW i7 is an all-electric, luxury sedan offering high performance and advanced technology. It features a 101.7 kWh battery pack, providing ranges of 560-625 km, and can accelerate 0-100 km/h in 3.7-4.7 seconds depending on the model.
